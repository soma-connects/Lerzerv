01Try this
  1. Client › Jobs › Fix leaking kitchen sink. Accept the price, pay into escrow, open chat and type a phone number.
  2. Switch to Artisan › My jobs. Start that job, then mark it complete.
  3. Client › Jobs › Done › Deep clean. Review it, then book Chinwe again.
  4. Artisan › My jobs › Replace bathroom tap. Send a price or choose Can’t take it.
  5. Client › Post. Post a job and see the “finding someone” state.
02Assumptions
  • Industry system: Barlow Condensed, square objects with registration marks, hairlines, one steel accent. Status is shown by label and fill, not by hue.
  • PROPOSAL: escrow and payment, earnings, Messages as a tab for artisans, “post a job for this artisan” in place of the dead-end Request form, and widen search. Paystack is not connected yet.
  • Names, counts and prices are sample data. Commission is 20%.
